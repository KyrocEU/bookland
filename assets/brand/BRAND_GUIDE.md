# Bookland brand guide

## Positioning

Bookland is a small macOS island for media, everyday tools, and optional Codex activity. It sits at the top edge of the screen and opens into an adaptive Liquid Glass panel. This release is an independent preview.

**Tagline:** A living island for your Mac.

**Short description:** Music, useful tools, and Codex activity in one hover island.

**Launch copy:** Meet Bookland, a lightweight macOS island that keeps your current song, focus timer, tools, and Codex activity within reach. Hover at the camera notch to open the panel; move away to get back to your desktop.

## Identity

The open book and small diamond are the Bookland mark. Use `bookland-icon-premium.png` or `Bookland.icns` for app distribution. The island uses a downscaled copy of that premium icon in its workspace menu, idle strip, and onboarding tour. Use `bookland-mark.svg` where a flat transparent mark is needed. Use `bookland-wordmark-light.svg` on dark backgrounds and `bookland-wordmark-dark.svg` on light backgrounds. Keep clear space around the mark at least one quarter of its width. Do not use the OpenAI Blossom as the Bookland logo.

| Color | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#050A13` | Dark canvas |
| Glass blue | `#5B95E9` | Accent and right page |
| Ice | `#D7F3FF` | Highlights |
| Text | `#F4F8FF` | Light wordmark |

Use the macOS system typeface for app UI. Use a clean sans-serif wordmark in marketing. Keep shadows soft and close to the surface; the island itself should remain translucent and let the real desktop influence the glass.
