# Bookland launch: next organic tests

Last updated 25 November 2026. This is a working campaign brief, not a claim about future reach.

## Current evidence

The first three Instagram Reels had 157, 45, and 99 plays when checked on 25 November. All three had zero likes, comments, saves, shares, and profile actions at that snapshot. Instagram reported that all viewers were non-followers. TikTok Studio showed the three public videos as visible to Everyone but zero views at that check. The sample is too small to identify a winning hook, and the accounts did not expose reliable three-second retention or average watch time. Recheck after a full day before diagnosing distribution or buying reach.

The public GitHub traffic API reported 14 repository views from 3 unique visitors and 94 clones from 53 unique cloners across its latest two-day window. Clone counts can include automation and are not a user-install count. The site has no first-party visitor count yet. The useful conversion signal is a real tester report, issue, or reproducible bug.

## Next content tests

Keep the chosen cadence of one new film per day. Each film should demonstrate one verified action in the *real* Bookland island. Use the 1080 × 1920 vertical export, inspect it in the opened Reel view, and keep important text in the phone safe area. The on-screen first frame must show the product, not an isolated logo.

1. **Codex answer at a glance:** Start with the actual island and a changing connected-turn state. Show the real result and compact close. Use the existing private EffectCraft project as the source, but improve the phone readability before publication. The current recording starts with Agents expanded, so label any separate hover-open shot as an edit. Do not imply that desktop-owned Codex approval requests route into Bookland.
2. **Music in one motion:** Show hover, a real 15-second rewind, the timestamp moving from 00:45 to 00:30, and close. The Music film published on 25 November is the baseline. Test a new, shorter first frame only after comparing its actual retention and interactions.
3. **Open-source first look:** Show the small island in use, the public source and the MIT license, then ask for one specific test. Avoid an unverified performance or compatibility claim.

Test different narrators deliberately. The six existing Fish Audio auditions provide female and male options; choose a voice for each concept after listening and checking the final mix. Keep narration rights, captions, and AI audio disclosures appropriate for each platform. Do not publish six near-identical edits just to test a voice.

End with one ask: **Comment BOOKLAND for the project link.** Reply manually until keyword delivery is verified. The public link is <https://kyroceu.github.io/bookland/> and the source is <https://github.com/KyrocEU/bookland>. Never promise an automatic DM that is not running.

## Community testing

r/macapps currently limits a developer to one app post per 30 days and routes newer accounts to The App Pile. Its [November 2026 moderation update](https://www.reddit.com/r/macapps/comments/1xo5ky1/rmacapps_moderation_update_phase_4_clearer_rules/) warns against repeat submissions and karma farming. The Bookland App Pile comment was removed by moderation. An earlier r/alphaandbetausers tester post and r/SideProject submissions were also removed by Reddit filters. These are not live community distribution. Do not submit another Bookland pitch to those communities while the account is new; use the public project page and GitHub first-look issue for tester feedback, and participate in relevant discussions only where there is a useful answer to give.

### Reusable tester copy for a permitted opportunity

**Title:** [macOS, Beta] Looking for Apple Silicon testers for Bookland's hover island

I'm building Bookland because I kept switching to YouTube just to replay 15 seconds. It sits beside the MacBook camera: hover to open media controls, tap rewind, then move away and it folds closed.

The current preview is free and MIT-licensed. It runs on Apple Silicon with macOS 26 or later. The build is locally signed and not Apple-notarized, so macOS may require Open Anyway on first launch.

The [download and source](https://github.com/KyrocEU/bookland) includes a 22-second real Mac demo showing a hover, a 15-second rewind (00:45 to 00:30), and the close.

Could someone with a compatible Mac check two things for me? Does the island open and close promptly, and does the 15-second seek work with your browser or player? If something fails, your Mac model, macOS version, and player name would help me reproduce it. Honest first-use feedback is welcome too.

## Review loop

For every live film, record publication URL and time, views, non-follower reach, three-second hold, average watch time, shares, saves, comments, profile actions, link clicks where measurable, and first-look reports. Mark metrics unavailable rather than substituting estimates. Compare each film after 24 hours and again after seven days. Use the findings to change the next hook or demo action. Do not buy a boost while the current creative has no interaction, and do not promise a view target.
