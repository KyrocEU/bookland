# Music rewind: next Bookland Reel

This is a production brief for a **new** 22-second, 9:16 Music Reel. It is not a published video. Use Bookland's recorded hover, real YouTube 15-second seek, and close footage from this directory. Keep the camera-area island and the playback time change legible on a phone. Do not substitute a mock interface for the action.

## Hook test

The local `ig-reel` hook heuristic ranked three distinct opening lines. These scores describe wording, not expected views.

| Spoken opening | Score | First-frame text |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Stop switching windows for a 15-second YouTube rewind. | 80.2 | **15 SECONDS BACK. ONE HOVER.** |
| Watch this Mac island rewind YouTube fifteen seconds. | 53.6 | **WATCH THE ISLAND REWIND.** |
| I missed one line. My Mac camera gave me fifteen seconds back. | 46.2 | **MISSED IT? GO BACK.** |

Use the first opening. Start on the real camera-area capture with movement in frame one. Show the actual rewind by second 11. The last line echoes “rewind” so the replay flows into the opening.

## Voiceover

At 165 words per minute, this is about 20 seconds, leaving roughly two seconds for the open/close sounds and final card. Record a natural, quick read with a curious first line, relief at the visible 0:45 → 0:30 result, and a warm invitation at the end. Compare a woman and a man before choosing the final voice; do not infer emotional quality from a voice label alone.

> Stop switching windows for a 15-second YouTube rewind.  
> Missed it?  
> Bookland lives beside my Mac camera.  
> I hover. It opens.  
> Tap back.  
> Same video, 0:45 to 0:30.  
> I see the change right there,  
> without hunting for the player.  
> I move away. Gone.  
> Free. Open source. GitHub.  
> Comment BOOKLAND for the link. Rewind, done.

## Edit plan

| Time | Picture and motion | On-screen copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3 s | Active cursor and closed island; begin the real hover immediately. No logo intro. | `15 SECONDS BACK` |
| 3–7 s | Real hover reveal at the camera. Reframe just enough to keep the island readable; preserve its natural proportions. | `HOVER BESIDE THE CAMERA` |
| 7–11 s | One continuous real `−15` click and the YouTube time moving from 0:45 to 0:30. Keep both control and result in frame. | `0:45 → 0:30` |
| 11–16 s | Keep the captured desktop visible and show the island closing after pointer exit. | `STAY IN YOUR WORK` |
| 16–22 s | Bookland logo and source link card. End with a clear comment prompt. | `COMMENT BOOKLAND` |

Keep every text card within the 9:16 safe area, away from Instagram/TikTok's right-side controls and bottom caption. Use short, recorded UI cues for the hover, click, and close; music must sit below the voice. Show the actual on-screen action before any generic feature list.
