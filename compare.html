Choose Bookland if...

You want a free MacBook notch app whose code you can inspect, build, change, and help improve. Bookland’s current release is a preview for Apple Silicon Macs on macOS 26.

Choose NotchNook Pro if...

You need its broader widget set, AI-agent approvals, or support for an older macOS release. Its Classic edition also runs on Intel Macs.

Choose Canopy if...

Lock-screen music and lyrics, AirPods status, and sending files from an iPhone to your Mac matter most to you.

THE CURRENT PREVIEW

What to know before you try Bookland.

Bookland is an independent project, not affiliated with NotchNook, Canopy, or Apple. It is free and open source, but its preview is not notarized. Calendar access depends on macOS permissions, Codex approval choices appear for connected turns, and browser playback does not yet detect every Chrome YouTube session.

We publish these limits so you can test the app with the right expectations. See the open issues, report a first impression, or help improve the source code.
