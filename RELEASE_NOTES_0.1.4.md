# Bookland 0.1.4 preview

Clicking the compact island now keeps its panel open until you close it. Hovering still opens the island immediately and closes it after the pointer leaves. This makes it easier to use music, Calendar, and Codex controls without holding the pointer over the panel.

On a Mac with YouTube playing in Chrome, the Music workspace displayed the live track and artwork. Its play/pause control and 15-second forward/back buttons changed the browser player; after a pause, Bookland's timeline synchronized to the stopped time. The connected Office calendar still showed its saved events after the desktop app was updated with the same local signing identity.

Bookland remains a free, open-source preview for Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. The ZIP is locally signed, not Apple-notarized. macOS may require **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** after the first launch attempt. Verify the ZIP with the companion SHA-256 file.

The [README](README.md) lists the current features and limits. In particular, approvals raised by turns already running in Codex Desktop cannot yet be answered inside Bookland; Bookland-connected turns can show their own approvals in the island.
